# Research and fairness standards

These rules apply to every candidate, every update and every contributor. They are also published on the site.

## Fairness rules

1. **Same method for everyone.** Every candidate is researched with the same method and to the same depth.
2. **Equal presentation.** Candidates are listed alphabetically by last name, with an identical card layout.
3. **Their words, our paraphrase.** Positions are paraphrased from the candidate's own statements, and every position has a source.
4. **No gaps, no guessing.** Where a candidate has not stated a position, we show "No stated position found." Where no public record items meet the rules below, we show "No items found."
5. **Public record is fact only.** It is limited to verifiable facts from at least 2 reputable sources, with the candidate's response where one exists.
6. **No opinions.** No endorsements, ratings, scores or pro/con labels.
7. **Open corrections.** Errors are corrected quickly and logged publicly.

## Public record rules

**Included:**
- Integrity commissioner findings and code-of-conduct rulings
- Court decisions and campaign finance rulings
- Key council votes and official decisions (for example, emergency declarations or uses of strong-mayor powers)
- Significant events reported by at least 2 reputable outlets

**Excluded:**
- Allegations that were never confirmed, and rumours
- Social media disputes
- Personal or family matters unrelated to public office
- Anything sourced only to opinion pieces or a single blog

Items are written in a factual tone, with a date and a source.

**Note:** incumbents naturally have a longer public record than first-time candidates. A longer record does not mean a candidate has done anything wrong.

## Sources

- Preferred sources: official City of Barrie records, candidates' own websites and statements, and established local news outlets.
- Each source is recorded with its title, outlet, URL and the date it was accessed.
- Where we found a source through BarrieVotes.ca and have not yet read it ourselves, the Sources page marks it "Found via BarrieVotes.ca".

## Data entry

- Positions record `status` as `stated` or `none_found`.
- Fact-check context (for example, city figures that differ from a candidate's claim) goes in the `note` column, never in the candidate's summary. Each note has its own sources, separate from the sources for the candidate's position.
